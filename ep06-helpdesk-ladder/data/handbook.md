## About us

Who we are: Dabbaloop is an invented meal-box delivery service in Pune. We cook vegetarian thalis in one central kitchen every morning and deliver them in a steel meal box to homes and offices in four zones of the city.

Delivery zones: Zone A is Kothrud, Karve Nagar, Erandwane and Deccan Gymkhana. Zone B is Baner, Aundh, Pashan and Balewadi. Zone C is Hinjewadi Phase 1, Hinjewadi Phase 2 and Wakad. Zone D is Viman Nagar, Kalyani Nagar and Kharadi.

## Meals and changes

Delivery slots: lunch comes Monday to Saturday, from 12:00 pm to 12:45 pm or 12:45 pm to 1:30 pm in Zones A and B, and from 1:00 pm to 1:45 pm in Zones C and D. Dinner comes Monday to Friday, from 7:30 pm to 8:15 pm or 8:15 pm to 9:00 pm. There is no dinner on Saturday and no delivery on Sunday.

Cut-off times: to skip, pause or change a meal, do it in the app before 9:30 am on the same day for lunch, or before 4:00 pm on the same day for dinner. A change made after the cut-off starts from the next meal, because your food is already cooked and packed.

Pausing a plan: you can pause your plan for up to 10 days in each calendar month, from My Plan, then Pause. Paused days are not lost; your plan end date moves forward by the same number of days.

Kitchen holidays: on a kitchen holiday there is no lunch and no dinner, in every zone. The meals of that day are added to the end of your plan automatically. The help desk keeps the list of kitchen holiday dates.

## Money

Delivery fee: delivery is free on every weekly, monthly and quarterly plan. A one-off order pays 30 rupees in Zones A and B and 45 rupees in Zones C and D, and it is free above 400 rupees.

The steel meal box: a new customer pays a refundable deposit of 350 rupees for two boxes, one with you and one being washed. Hand back the empty box to the rider at your next delivery. When you leave, the deposit comes back within 7 working days after both boxes are returned.

Missed meal: if your meal has not arrived 45 minutes after your slot ends, it counts as missed. Report it in the app within 6 hours of the end of the slot and the full meal is credited to your wallet.

## Help and complaints

Support hours: in-app chat is open from 8:00 am to 10:00 pm, Monday to Saturday, and from 10:00 am to 2:00 pm on Sunday. You can also ask for a call-back in the chat; an agent calls you within 30 minutes during chat hours.

Complaints: a support agent replies within 15 minutes during chat hours, and most complaints are closed within 48 hours. A complaint that is still open after 48 hours goes to the Customer Happiness Lead, and after 5 working days the Head of Operations reviews it.

Food safety: if anything in your meal looks, smells or tastes wrong, stop eating it. Keep the food and the box, take a photo and report it within 2 hours of delivery. The kitchen team calls you back within 1 hour.
