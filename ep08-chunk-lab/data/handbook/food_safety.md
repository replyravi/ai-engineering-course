# Dabbaloop Kitchen Food Safety and Allergen Guide

Version 2.0, October 2026. Issued by the Dabbaloop kitchen team. Dabbaloop is an invented company used for teaching; every rule here is made up.

## 1. Our kitchen

All Dabbaloop meals are cooked in one central kitchen in Kothrud, Pune. Lunch cooking starts at 7:30 am and every lunch box is packed and sealed by 11:15 am. Dinner cooking starts at 4:30 pm, after the dinner cut-off for changes has passed.

## 2. Hygiene rules we follow every day

- Every cook and packer wears a hair cap, gloves and an apron, and washes hands every 30 minutes and after handling raw vegetables.
- Fridge and freezer temperatures are checked and written in a log three times a day. Fridges must stay at 5 degrees C or colder.
- Drinking and cooking water is RO purified and tested every month by an outside lab.
- Frying oil is used for one day only and then thrown away. We never top up old oil.
- Every steel meal box is washed in water at 80 degrees C and air dried before reuse.
- The whole kitchen is deep cleaned every Sunday, which is why there are no Sunday deliveries.

## 3. Allergens in our food

Our menu labels show five allergens. Milk: paneer, curd, ghee and butter. Gluten: wheat phulkas and some sabzis thickened with wheat flour. Peanuts: sabudana, poha and several Maharashtrian sabzis. Sesame: til chutney and some gravies. Tree nuts: the cashew gravy that is usually served on Fridays.

Our kitchen is not nut-free. Peanuts and cashews are used in the same kitchen as every other dish, so we cannot promise that any meal is free from traces of nuts. If a nut allergy is severe, please do not rely on our meals.

Jain thalis are cooked in separate vessels marked with a green band, but in the same kitchen and by the same team.

## 4. Allergy notes and red stickers

If you add an allergy note under Profile, then Food preferences, the packer checks your meal against the menu of the day and puts a red sticker on the bag. The red sticker means we have read your note. It is not a medical guarantee.

## 5. Labels on the lid

Every meal box lid has a printed label with the date, the meal (lunch or dinner), the thali name and the allergens in that day's menu.

## 6. Storing and reheating at home

- Eat your meal within 3 hours of delivery. If you will eat later, move the food to the fridge within 1 hour of delivery and finish it the same day.
- Never put the steel box in a microwave. Move the food to a microwave-safe bowl and heat it until it is steaming hot all the way through.
- Curd and raita should not stay out of the fridge for more than 2 hours, and for less than 1 hour on hot summer days.
- Do not keep leftovers for the next day's lunch.

## 7. If something seems wrong

If the food smells sour, looks unusual or the seal on the lid is broken, do not eat it. T ake a photo, keep the food and report it in the app within 2 hours of delivery. The kitchen team calls you back within 1 hour, and the meal is credited in full.

## 8. Kitchen visits

Customers can visit the kitchen on the first Saturday of every month between 3:00 pm and 5:00 pm. Book a visit in the app. Each group can have up to 6 people, and children under 12 must come with an adult.
