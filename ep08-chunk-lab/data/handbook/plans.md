# Dabbaloop Meal Plans and Prices

Prices for 2026-27, valid from 1 April 2026. All prices are in Indian rupees and include 5 percent GST. Everything here is invented for teaching.

## The thalis

Every thali is vegetarian and cooked fresh in our central kitchen each morning.

| Thali | What is inside | One meal | Monthly plan (22 meals) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ghar Ka Khana | 3 phulkas, 1 sabzi, dal, rice, salad | ₹120 | ₹2,490 |
| Jain Thali | No onion, garlic or root vegetables, 3 phulkas, sabzi, dal, rice | ₹130 | ₹2,690 |
| Protein Plus | Paneer or soya sabzi, sprouts, extra dal, less rice, 2 phulkas | ₹160 | ₹3,290 |
| Mini Meal | 2 phulkas, sabzi, small dal and rice, for kids and light eaters | ₹90 | ₹1,850 |

Add-ons for any thali: an extra phulka for ₹10, a bowl of curd for ₹20, buttermilk for ₹20 and the sweet of the day for ₹30.

## Plan lengths

- Weekly: 5 lunches (Monday to Friday) or 6 lunches (Monday to Saturday).
- Monthly: 22 meals, lunch or dinner.
- Quarterly: 66 meals, with 8 percent off the monthly price.

Lunch and dinner are separate plans. If you want both, you buy two plans and the second one gets 5 percent off.

## Trial meal

A first-time customer can order one trial Ghar Ka Khana thali for ₹79. The trial is limited to one per phone number and one per delivery address.

## Pausing your plan

You can pause your plan for up to 10 days in each calendar month. Pause from the app under My Plan, then Pause, before the cut-off time (9:30 am for lunch, 4:00 pm for dinner). Paused days are not lost: your plan end date moves forward by the same number of days.

If you need a pause longer than 10 days, for example for a trip home during Diwali, write to support and we will freeze your plan instead.

## Upgrading or downgrading

You can switch between thalis once a week. The new thali starts from the next Monday, and the price difference for the remaining meals is added to your next bill or credited to your wallet.

## Spice level and food preferences

Choose mild, regular or spicy under Profile, then Food preferences. Allergy notes go in the same place, and the kitchen puts a red sticker on any meal box with an allergy note. Read the Food Safety and Allergen Guide before you rely on an allergy note.

## Paying for your plan

We accept UPI, debit and credit cards, and net banking. Monthly and quarterly plans can renew on UPI AutoPay; you get a reminder 2 days before every renewal. We do not accept cash on delivery for subscriptions.

## Office plans

Offices with 15 or more people in one building can ask for an office plan, with 10 percent off and a single drop point. Office plans are set up by the corporate team, not in the app.
