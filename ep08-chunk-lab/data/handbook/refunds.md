# Dabbaloop Refunds and Credits Policy

Version 3.2, effective 1 August 2026. Dabbaloop is an invented meal-box delivery service used for teaching; every name, price and rule in this handbook is made up.

## Who this policy covers

This policy applies to every Dabbaloop customer in Pune, whether you pay for a weekly plan, a monthly plan, a quarterly plan or a single one-off thali. Corporate office plans follow the same rules unless the office contract says otherwise.

## Missed or late meal

A meal counts as missed when it has not reached you 45 minutes after your delivery slot ends. For example, if your lunch slot is 12:45 pm to 1:30 pm and nothing has arrived by 2:15 pm, that lunch is missed.

Report a missed lunch or dinner within 6 hours of the end of your slot. Use Orders, then Report a problem, in the Dabbaloop app. We add a full credit for that meal to your Dabbaloop Wallet, usually within 2 hours of your report. Reports made after 6 hours are reviewed one by one and a credit is not guaranteed.

If the same address has three missed meals in one calendar month, the operations team calls you to fix the cause, for example a wrong gate number or a society that does not let riders in.

## Wrong or damaged meal

If you receive the wrong plan (for example a regular thali instead of a Jain thali) or the box arrives spilled or broken, send a photo through the app within 2 hours of delivery. You can choose a replacement meal in the next slot or a 100 percent credit to your wallet. You do not need to return the food.

## Taste and temperature complaints

Food that is cold, too salty or not up to the mark gets a 50 percent wallet credit for that meal. This credit is limited to one claim per week per subscription, because every meal is cooked fresh the same morning and we fix kitchen issues quickly.

## Cancelling a subscription

You can cancel a subscription at any time from My Plan, then Cancel plan.

- Monthly and quarterly plans cancelled within the first 3 days after the start date get a full refund.
- After the first 3 days, the unused meals are refunded pro rata, minus a processing fee of ₹149.
- Weekly plans cannot be refunded once the week has started, but the unused meals become wallet credit.

Bank refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 working days. If you prefer, we can add the amount to your Dabbaloop Wallet instantly instead.

## Dabbaloop Wallet credits

Wallet credits are used automatically on your next payment. Credits expire 90 days after they are added. Wallet balance cannot be withdrawn as cash, except when you close your account, when any balance above ₹100 is paid back to your bank account.

## What we cannot refund

- Festival special orders (for example Diwali faral boxes) after their order cut-off has passed.
- The delivery fee on a one-off order once the rider has picked it up.
- Meals you paused or skipped after the cut-off time. Pause rules are in the Meal Plans handbook.

The steel meal box deposit is not a refund. It is returned separately when you hand back your boxes; see the Delivery handbook.
