About Kadak Kettle

Kadak Kettle is a small tea counter next to a busy city bus stop. It’s a standing counter with six stools, one big steel kettle and a glass case full of snacks.

How we started

We began with one gas stove and two recipes: masala chai and vada pav. Today the menu has twelve items, and most of them still cost less than ₹50.

What we care about

  • Ginger crushed fresh every morning
  • Milk delivered twice a day
  • Snacks fried in small batches, never kept overnight

Order ahead

Running late for your bus? Send the counter your order and your name, and skip the line when you arrive. We’ll keep your order ready at the counter.

The people behind the counter

The counter is run by a family of four. One brother makes the tea, one sister fries the snacks, and their parents handle the money and the morning rush.
