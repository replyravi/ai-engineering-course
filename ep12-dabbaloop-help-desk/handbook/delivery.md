# Dabbaloop Delivery Handbook

Zones, delivery slots, cut-off times and the steel meal box. Updated 15 September 2026. All details are invented for teaching.

## Delivery zones

We deliver in four zones of Pune. Your zone decides your delivery slot and the fee for one-off orders.

- Zone A: Kothrud, Karve Nagar, Erandwane and Deccan Gymkhana.
- Zone B: Baner, Aundh, Pashan and Balewadi.
- Zone C: Hinjewadi Phase 1, Hinjewadi Phase 2 and Wakad.
- Zone D: Viman Nagar, Kalyani Nagar and Kharadi.

Hinjewadi Phase 3 joins Zone C as a pilot from 1 November 2026. We do not deliver to Hadapsar, Pimpri or Chinchwad yet. You can join the waiting list in the app.

## Delivery slots

Lunch slots, Monday to Saturday:

- Zones A and B: 12:00 pm to 12:45 pm, or 12:45 pm to 1:30 pm.
- Zones C and D: 1:00 pm to 1:45 pm.

Dinner slots, Monday to Friday, all zones: 7:30 pm to 8:15 pm, or 8:15 pm to 9:00 pm.

There is no dinner on Saturday. The kitchen is closed on Sunday, so there are no Sunday deliveries, except Sunday Special pre-orders announced in the app.

## Cut-off times for changes

To skip, pause or change a meal, make the change in the app before the cut-off:

- Lunch: 9:30 am on the same day.
- Dinner: 4:00 pm on the same day.

Changes made after the cut-off apply from the next meal, because the kitchen has already cooked and packed your meal.

## Delivery fee

Delivery is free for every weekly, monthly and quarterly plan. One-off orders pay ₹30 in Zones A and B, and ₹45 in Zones C and D. One-off orders above ₹400 are delivered free in every zone.

## The steel meal box

Meals come in a four-compartment steel meal box inside an insulated bag. A new customer pays a refundable deposit of ₹350 for two boxes, because one box is with you while the other is being washed.

Hand the empty box back to the rider at your next delivery. Please rinse it, but you do not need to wash it with soap; the kitchen sterilises every box. A box that is lost or dented beyond use is charged at ₹350 from the deposit. The deposit is returned within 7 working days after you return both boxes when you leave.

## Late or missed meal

The app shows your rider's live location from the moment your meal leaves the kitchen. If the meal still has not arrived 45 minutes after your slot ends, it is treated as missed. Report it in the app within 6 hours of the slot ending to get a full wallet credit. The full rules are in the Refunds and Credits Policy.

## Rain, traffic and bandh days

During a heavy rain alert from the weather department, slots can shift by up to 60 minutes and the app sends you a notice. On a city-wide bandh day, deliveries are cancelled and every affected meal is credited to your wallet automatically, with no report needed.

## Gated societies and offices

If security does not let riders in, the rider leaves the meal box at the main gate with your flat number and name on the bag, and sends a photo in the app. Add gate instructions under Profile, then Address notes. Offices can choose a single drop point, such as the reception desk.
