# Dabbaloop Help, Complaints and Contact

How to reach us, how complaints are handled and quick answers to common questions. Updated 1 October 2026. The company and its contacts are invented for teaching.

## Support hours

- In-app chat: 8:00 am to 10:00 pm, Monday to Saturday, and 10:00 am to 2:00 pm on Sunday.
- Email: support@dabbaloop.example, answered within 24 hours, every day.
- Call-back: request one in the app during chat hours and an agent calls you within 30 minutes.

In-app chat is the fastest way to reach us, because the agent can already see your orders and address.

## How we handle a complaint

1. You raise it in chat, by email or with Report a problem on an order.
2. A support agent replies within 15 minutes during chat hours.
3. Most complaints are closed within 48 hours.
4. If yours is not closed in 48 hours, it moves to the Customer Happiness Lead.
5. If it is still open after 5 working days, the Head of Operations reviews it personally.

You get a complaint number at step 1. Quote it in every message and you will never need to explain twice.

## Food safety concerns

If anything in your meal box looks, smells or tastes wrong, stop eating it. Keep the food and the box, take a photo and report it within 2 hours of delivery. Our kitchen team calls you back within 1 hour. If anyone feels unwell, see a doctor first; we will cover the meal and talk to you once you are okay. The Food Safety and Allergen Guide explains how the kitchen works.

## Quick answers

**My lunch did not come. What do I do?**
Wait until 45 minutes after your slot ends, then use Report a problem in the app. You have 6 hours from the end of the slot to report it and get the full meal credited to your wallet.

**How do I pause my meals?**
Open My Plan, then Pause, before 9:30 am for lunch or 4:00 pm for dinner. You can pause up to 10 days a month, and your plan end date moves forward.

**Can I change my delivery address?**
Yes, under Profile, then Addresses. A new address in a different zone may change your slot. Changes before the cut-off apply to the next meal.

**Can someone else receive my meal box?**
Yes. Add their name under Address notes so the rider knows whom to hand it to.

## Your account and your data

We use your phone number and address only to deliver your food and contact you about your orders. We share your address and first name only with the rider on the day. To delete your account, write to support; we finish the deletion within 30 days and send you a confirmation email.

## Feedback

Rate each meal from 1 to 5 stars in the app. The kitchen reads every rating below 3 stars the same evening.
