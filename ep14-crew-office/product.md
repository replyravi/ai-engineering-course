# Pickup Parrot

Invented product for a course demo. Pickup Parrot is not a real company, app or service.

- What it does: sends your customers a short message when their order, sample or class is ready, and keeps one shared list of pre-orders
- Messages: by SMS or email, in English, Hindi, Marathi or Malayalam
- Set-up: about 15 minutes, no new hardware
- Free trial: 30 days, no card needed
- Price after the trial: Rs 499 a month for each location
- Sender of our outreach emails: Neha Rao, partnerships, Pickup Parrot
- Outreach rule: one email per business, and no follow-up unless they reply
