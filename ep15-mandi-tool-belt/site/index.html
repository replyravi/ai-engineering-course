Mandi Tool Belt: how to read the sample price board

The Mandi Tool Belt is a teaching project from the AI Engineering course by Ravi Shanker Singh (TechWithRavi). Everything on these three pages is invented for the course. The prices, charges and rules are not the rates or rules of any real market or market committee.

What the price board shows

The board lists one price a day for five vegetables: onion, tomato, potato, cabbage and brinjal. It covers three cities, Nashik, Pune and Indore, over ten sample days. Day 1 is the first sample day. The days are not calendar dates.

Each price is the modal price in rupees per kilogram. The modal price is the price at which most of that day's trade happened, so a few lots always sell above or below it.

Arrivals tell you how much of a vegetable reached the market that day. They are counted in quintals, and one quintal is 100 kilograms.

Questions the agent answers

The agent answers price questions from the board, explains the charges a buyer pays on top of the price, and converts between bags, crates and kilograms. The charges are on the Buyer charges page. Bag and crate sizes are on the Bags, crates and quintals page.

Rounding

Every bill is worked out to the paisa. It is rounded to the nearest rupee only at the very end, when the buyer pays.
