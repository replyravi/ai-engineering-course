Bags, crates and quintals

Invented for the AI Engineering course. Real markets use many different bag sizes.

How much one bag or crate holds

  • Onion: one bag holds 50 kg.
  • Potato: one bag holds 50 kg.
  • Tomato: one crate holds 25 kg.
  • Cabbage: one sack holds 40 kg.
  • Brinjal: one crate holds 20 kg.

The quintal

Arrivals and bulk orders are counted in quintals. One quintal is 100 kg, so one quintal of onions is two bags. To turn quintals into kilograms, multiply by 100.

Grades

The price board shows grade A, the main quality of the day. Grade B lots are smaller or slightly damaged and usually sell for less. The board does not list grade B prices.
